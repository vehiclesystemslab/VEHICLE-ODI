## Pre-submission notes

- The manuscript is framed as a Hypothesis and Theory article, not as an operational product.
- The paper avoids claiming that VEHICLE-ODI replaces SSA, STM, astrodynamics or active debris removal.
- The proposed OTI is described as experimental and model-dependent.
- A1-A6 regimes are clearly presented as research classifications requiring validation.
- Operational intervention, ownership, liability and legal permission are explicitly excluded from automatic decision-making.
- Statements included: author contributions, conflict of interest, funding, data availability, ethics statement and acknowledgments.
